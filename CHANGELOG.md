# Changelog

## 1.3.1

- Phone sign-in: the "Code" label no longer overlaps the field border on the verification step
- Phone sign-in page shows the Sidekick name

## 1.3.0

- New name, logo, launcher icon and TV banner
- Fix: after leaving a stream, focus could get stuck on the side menu and bounce back to Home
- Picking the stream that's playing in picture-in-picture expands it instantly, without reloading
- Automatic updates setting: check every time the app opens (default), once a day, or off
- Your Kick sign-in is now stored encrypted
- About screen with privacy and license info
- Open source under the MIT license

## 1.2.0

- Fixes "HTTP 429" errors when signed in
- Clips: top clips on Home and popular clips on channel pages
- Live on Kick row and Play Next on the Android TV home screen
- Alerts when someone you follow goes live
- 7TV emotes and chat filters
- Multi-view: watch up to 4 streams at once
- Picture-in-picture, sleep timer and loudness equalization
- Continue watching and Recently watched on Home

## 1.1.0

- Chat badges now show Kick's icons (mod, VIP, OG, founder, subscriber, gifter, global badges)
- Chat slides in and out smoothly; the video resizes to make room
- Unfollowing asks for confirmation
- In-app updates from GitHub Releases (Settings → Check for updates)
- Animations throughout the app and a launch screen
- Sign-in fixes and Android 16 back-button support

## 1.0.0

- First release: live streams, past broadcasts, chat, live rewind, following and sign-in
